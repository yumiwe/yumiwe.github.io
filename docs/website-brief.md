# Yumiwe Business Context

## What Yumiwe Is

Yumiwe is building a new system for how people and opportunities find one another.

The employment market currently provides poor experiences on both sides.

Candidates search through large numbers of job postings, attempt to determine where they fit, repeatedly rewrite résumés and applications, complete repetitive forms, and submit applications into systems that provide little visibility or feedback.

Recruiters and hiring teams face the inverse problem. Publishing a position can produce an overwhelming number of applications of widely varying relevance and quality. Recruiters rely on applicant tracking systems, keyword searches, filters, sourcing tools, manual review and outbound outreach to find promising candidates.

Technology has made both sides faster without necessarily making the underlying matching process better. Easier applications create more applications. More applications create more automated filtering. More filtering encourages candidates to optimize for filters and submit more applications.

Yumiwe intends to change the system rather than simply automate one side of it.

## Candidate Side

Yumiwe begins with the individual.

Instead of treating a résumé as the canonical representation of a person, Yumiwe maintains a much richer understanding of their working life.

This can include their:

- employment and project history;
- accomplishments and evidence;
- skills and capabilities;
- responsibilities;
- domain knowledge;
- preferences;
- interests;
- career direction;
- constraints;
- and other context relevant to understanding their professional experience.

A résumé is a representation derived from this underlying career record for a particular opportunity. It is not the record itself.

The initial Yumiwe product uses this understanding to help candidates evaluate opportunities and produce strong, evidence-grounded applications tailored to specific roles without inventing experience or reducing the person to keyword optimization.

Candidates should be able to use Yumiwe throughout their working lives rather than only when actively looking for a job.

Candidate access is intended to remain free.

## Recruiter and Employer Side

Yumiwe is ultimately a two-sided employment platform.

Recruiters and employers represent the primary monetization opportunity.

Instead of receiving an undifferentiated pile of résumés and attempting to reconstruct candidate quality from documents, employers should be able to use Yumiwe to understand and discover relevant candidates more directly.

The recruiter-side product may eventually include capabilities such as:

- hosting job opportunities;
- receiving candidate applications;
- intelligent application ingestion;
- candidate evaluation and filtering;
- candidate discovery;
- natural-language or structured queries over candidate populations;
- candidate sourcing;
- matching candidates to opportunities;
- identifying potentially strong candidates who have not applied;
- recruiter outreach;
- screening and workflow support;
- and tools for managing the process from discovery through hiring.

Candidates may enter this ecosystem in multiple ways.

A candidate might already use Yumiwe and choose to participate in an opportunity.

A candidate might encounter Yumiwe when applying for a job hosted through the platform and provide their résumé or career information at that point.

Recruiters may also use Yumiwe for candidate discovery and sourcing.

The exact recruiter product and interaction model are still being designed. Do not assume that the current implementation represents the final workflow.

## Business Model

The intended economic alignment is important.

Yumiwe does not intend to monetize candidates for creating résumés, maintaining their career record, or receiving core career assistance.

The candidate side is intended to remain free.

Revenue is expected primarily from recruiters and employers who pay for capabilities associated with hiring, such as job hosting, application intake, candidate discovery, sourcing, search/query, matching, outreach and related recruiting workflows.

Specific pricing, packaging and commercial models have not yet been determined.

Do not invent pricing tiers, subscription plans or commercial commitments that have not been explicitly defined.

## The Larger Thesis

Yumiwe is not a résumé generator.

Résumé generation is an initial product and a useful entry point into a much larger problem.

Yumiwe's ambition is to improve the infrastructure through which people and work find one another.

Today, candidates and recruiters operate through disconnected representations of the same underlying problem.

Candidates attempt to describe themselves through résumés and application forms.

Employers attempt to describe opportunities through job descriptions.

Software then attempts to match these relatively poor representations using search, keywords, filters, ranking systems and increasingly AI-generated content.

Yumiwe's long-term opportunity is to understand both sides more deeply.

On one side:

**Who is this person, what have they actually done, what can they do, what do they want, and where might they thrive?**

On the other:

**What does this organization actually need, what work needs to be done, and what kind of person is likely to succeed there?**

If both sides can be represented with greater fidelity, the system can facilitate better discovery and matching before either party has to fight through the traditional application funnel.

The objective is not simply to make applying for jobs faster.

It is to make the existing high-volume application model less necessary.

## Product Principles

### The person is larger than their résumé

The career record is the underlying source of truth. Résumés, applications and other representations are derived artifacts created for particular purposes.

### Evidence matters

Yumiwe should represent candidates accurately. The system must not fabricate experience, accomplishments, skills or qualifications simply to improve apparent fit.

### Candidates are people, not inventory

The candidate-side product should work on behalf of the individual and respect their agency, privacy and preferences.

### Better matching beats more applications

Success is not measured by helping someone submit the largest possible number of applications.

The goal is better opportunities, better representation and better matches.

### Both sides of the market matter

Improving candidate tooling while simply overwhelming recruiters with more generated applications does not solve the employment problem.

Yumiwe should ultimately improve the system for candidates, recruiters and employers together.

### AI is infrastructure, not the brand

Yumiwe makes extensive use of AI and intelligent systems.

It should not position itself primarily as an "AI company," "AI résumé builder," or similar category.

The technology enables the experience. It is not the identity of the company.

## Current Stage

Yumiwe is early-stage.

The initial candidate-side product is under active development.

The broader recruiter/employer platform is part of the company vision but is not yet fully designed or implemented.

Public website content must clearly distinguish between:

- what exists today;
- what is currently being built;
- and the longer-term vision.

Do not present planned capabilities as currently available.

## Brand

The company and product are called:

**Yumiwe**

Do not explain, decode or manufacture a public meaning for the name.

Do not create taglines or brand stories based on the spelling of Yumiwe unless explicitly requested.

The name should be allowed to become its own brand.

## Ambition

Yumiwe should be treated as a company attempting to rethink a large existing market, not as a résumé-generation utility.

The initial product should be excellent in its own right, but architectural, product and brand decisions should avoid unnecessarily constraining Yumiwe to résumé generation.

The long-term ambition is to build a substantially better system for connecting people with work.

## V1 Website Purpose

The current Yumiwe website is an early-stage company brochure, not the finished product website.

Its purpose is to establish Yumiwe as a credible company, communicate the problem and company thesis, demonstrate that a real product is being built, introduce the team, and provide a public presence for partners and programs evaluating the company.

This version of the website may be seen by potential partners, platform providers, startup programs, future investors, candidates, recruiters and other people evaluating Yumiwe.

The website should be truthful about the company's current stage.

It should not attempt to look like a mature SaaS company when Yumiwe is still early.

## Likely V1 Content

The initial site should be capable of supporting:

- a minimal hero introducing Yumiwe and its core idea;
- a concise explanation of the problem Yumiwe is addressing;
- an introduction to the candidate-side product currently being built;
- enough of the broader recruiter/employer vision to establish that Yumiwe is larger than a résumé-generation product;
- an embedded product demonstration video;
- a small team section with photographs, names, roles and concise biographies;
- basic company/contact information;
- privacy and other required legal pages when needed.

Do not add lead-generation forms, pricing tables, customer logos, testimonials, fabricated metrics, waitlists or other conversion mechanisms unless explicitly requested.

There is currently no need for an email capture or signup form.

## Product Claims

Some strategically important capabilities and commercial/product architecture are still under development or are not yet intended for public announcement.

Do not infer or publicly advertise implementation details, partnerships, integrations, platform relationships, pricing models or future capabilities merely because they appear in internal business context.

Public copy should distinguish clearly between what exists, what is being built, and the broader company vision.

## Design Direction

Yumiwe should feel:

- human;
- calm;
- approachable;
- optimistic;
- intelligent;
- confident without being boastful;
- slightly playful without being childish.

The site should be light and spacious.

Use generous whitespace. Individual ideas should have room to breathe. Prefer a small number of strong elements over visual density.

Typography should do much of the visual work.

The initial palette should be restrained: a warm or neutral light background, very dark readable text, and a small number of carefully chosen identity colours.

Do not choose a final brand palette merely because one is required to implement the first version. Colour exploration is part of the branding process.

## What Yumiwe Should Not Look Like

Avoid stereotypical AI aesthetics such as neon gradients, glowing spheres, circuit imagery, excessive sparkles, futuristic dark interfaces or visual language intended primarily to communicate "AI."

Avoid generic enterprise SaaS design such as dense feature-card grids, excessive badges and pills, floating dashboard screenshots, gratuitous gradients and sections created merely to make the page appear substantial.

Avoid conventional recruiting imagery such as handshakes, résumé icons, briefcases, targets and generic office stock photography.

Avoid marketing language such as "revolutionary," "AI-powered," "unlock your potential," "supercharge your career," and similar generic technology claims unless specifically approved.

Yumiwe should not look like software trying to convince the visitor that it is software.

## Technology

V1 should remain deliberately simple.

Use static HTML, CSS and minimal browser-native JavaScript hosted through GitHub Pages.

Prefer semantic HTML and maintainable CSS.

Do not introduce a JavaScript framework, CSS framework, package manager, build pipeline or external runtime dependency unless a concrete requirement justifies it.

The website should work without JavaScript wherever practical.

The source should remain easy for both humans and coding agents to understand and modify.

The architecture may change when the full Yumiwe product website is developed. V1 should not prematurely solve for that future system.
