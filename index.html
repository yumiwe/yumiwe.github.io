The work you're looking for is looking for you.

The problem

Finding work has become a numbers game.

People apply to more jobs. Employers receive more applications.

Candidates learn to satisfy filters. Employers add more filters.

Résumés are optimized to get noticed, not necessarily to be understood.

Everyone is doing more, and understanding less.

Our observation

A résumé isn't the person.

A job description isn't the job.

Yet we've built the hiring system around matching one to the other.

A résumé is a compressed version of someone's experience. A job description is a compressed version of what a team needs.

Both are useful.

Neither is the thing we're actually trying to understand.

Yumiwe does it differently

Understand the person.

Understand the work.

Yumiwe starts with what sits behind the résumé and the job description.

What has this person actually done? What are they good at? What do they want to do next?

What does this team actually need? What work needs to be done? What really matters — and what doesn't?

When you understand both, you can make better connections between them.

Where we start

We start with the person.

Yumiwe builds an understanding of your experience — not just the parts that made it onto your last résumé.

What you've done. What you're good at. What matters to you. And what you want to do next.

See a job that looks interesting? Give it to Yumiwe. We'll help you understand whether it's actually a good fit — and tell you when we think it isn't.

If it's worth pursuing, Yumiwe helps you put your best, truthful case forward.

And every conversation helps Yumiwe understand you better.

It belongs to you

Your career record belongs to you.

Yumiwe learns about your experience so it can work for you — not so you can become inventory for someone else.

You decide what Yumiwe knows. You decide what gets shared. And you decide when you want to be discovered.

The person stays in control.

Where this goes

The résumé is only the beginning.

Today, Yumiwe helps people navigate a hiring system built around résumés, job descriptions and applications.

But as Yumiwe comes to understand both people and work, those documents no longer need to sit between them.

The opportunity is not to make the old process faster. It's to need less of the old process at all.
